Kage no Hana (Shadow Flower)
A tale of memory, silence, and the flowers that bloom in darkness

In a quiet town where shadows remember what people forget, a young girl discovers she can see the "flowers" that grow from people's hidden emotions. This is her story.

Chapter 01 The Girl Who Saw Shadows

The Girl Who Saw Shadows

In the quiet town of Hikarimachi, where the cherry blossoms fell like pink snow even in summer, there lived a girl named Aiko who could see things others could not.

She saw flowers blooming from people's chests — dark, beautiful flowers that represented their hidden emotions. No one else could see them. Only her.

Chapter 02 The Shadow That Smiled

The Shadow That Smiled

One evening, Aiko met a boy whose shadow had no flower. Instead, it smiled at her when no one was looking. That was the day everything changed.

His name was Ren. He carried a silence so deep that even the wind seemed afraid to touch him.

Chapter 03 Flowers That Cry

Flowers That Cry

When Aiko finally touched Ren's hand, she saw the truth. His flower wasn't missing — it was buried deep inside his chest, crying silently for years.

That night, the first real cherry blossom fell in Hikarimachi after ten long summers.
