Kage no Hana

影の花 — An Original Japanese Light Novel

Synopsis

In the quiet town of Mizukage, emotions do not stay hidden inside the heart. They bloom as flowers only Aiko can see — radiant sunflowers of joy, fragile bluebells of sorrow, fierce camellias of anger.

Then one ordinary afternoon, she notices something new. Some shadows smile back.

This is the story of Aiko and Ren, and the legendary Kage no Hana — the Shadow Flower that blooms only where light and darkness learn to hold each other.
