Kage no Hana

影の花 — An Original Japanese Light Novel

In a town where emotions bloom as flowers only she can see, a quiet girl named Aiko discovers that some shadows smile back. This is her story.

CHAPTER 01

The Girl Who Saw Shadows

In Hikarimachi, Aiko could see flowers blooming from people's chests — beautiful, dark flowers that showed their hidden emotions.

CHAPTER 02

The Shadow That Smiled

One evening she met Ren. His shadow had no flower. Instead, it smiled at her when no one else was looking. His silence was deeper than the night.

CHAPTER 03

Flowers That Cry

When Aiko finally touched his hand, she understood. His flower wasn't missing — it was buried deep inside his chest, crying silently for years.

CHAPTER 04

The Night the Blossoms Returned

For the first time in ten years, the cherry blossoms bloomed in summer. Aiko finally understood what Ren had been protecting all along.

CHAPTER 05

The Flower That Chose to Bloom

In the end, Aiko made a choice that would change both their lives forever. Some flowers only bloom when you stop being afraid of the dark.
